About me

I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University and my bachelor's degree in Statistics from Shandong University.

Research interests

1

Smallest singular values of random matrices

I am interested in deviation inequalities for the smallest singular value and the invertibility of random matrices. Questions about sharp lower-tail estimates, arithmetic structure, and extensions from independent-entry models to dependent or structured ensembles are particularly appealing to me.

2

Moments and tails of random chaoses

I am interested in moment and tail estimates for Gaussian and more general polynomial chaoses, including models with log-concave tails. I would like to better understand how the geometry of coefficient tensors determines concentration, particularly beyond the sub-Gaussian setting.

3

Sparse recovery under weak assumptions

I am interested in sparse recovery under weak moment assumptions, including the small-ball method and empirical-process techniques. A central goal is to obtain near-optimal recovery guarantees for structured measurements without relying on strong tail assumptions.
